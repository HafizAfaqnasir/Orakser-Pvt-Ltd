Overview

Put Your Ownership Beyond Doubt

Copyright protects original creative works the moment they're created, but formal registration gives you an official, dated public record of ownership — invaluable if your work is ever copied or disputed.

Orakser assists authors, designers, software developers, musicians and businesses in registering books, software, designs, photography, marketing content and more with the Copyright Office of Pakistan.

Key Benefits

  • Official, dated proof of authorship and ownership
  • Stronger legal standing in infringement disputes
  • Easier licensing and commercialization of your work
  • Covers literary, artistic, musical and software works
  • Peace of mind before publishing or launching

Before You Apply

What You'll Need

  • Copy or sample of the work (manuscript, artwork, code, etc.)
  • CNIC copy of the author/owner
  • Date of creation and, if published, date of first publication
  • Details of any co-authors or assignees
  • Business proof, if the work is company-owned
