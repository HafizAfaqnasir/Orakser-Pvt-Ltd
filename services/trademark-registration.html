Overview

Protect the Name Customers Already Trust

A registered trademark stops competitors from copying your brand name, logo or slogan — and gives you the legal standing to act immediately if they do. In Pakistan, trademarks are examined and registered by the Intellectual Property Organization (IPO Pakistan).

Orakser has handled trademark filings across all 45 classes since 2010, including for restaurants, education brands, manufacturers and service businesses. We manage the entire process so you can stay focused on running your business.

Key Benefits

  • Exclusive legal right to use your brand name/logo
  • Grounds to stop copycats and counterfeiters
  • Use of the ® symbol once registered
  • An intangible asset you can license or sell
  • Stronger standing for import/export and franchising
Our Process

How We Handle Your Trademark

1
Clearance Search
We search existing marks to confirm your brand name is available.
2
Filing
We file your application with IPO Pakistan in the correct class(es).
3
Examination & Publication
We respond to any objections and manage journal publication.
4
Certificate
Once unopposed, your registration certificate is issued.
Full registration typically takes 18–24 months; protection applies from your filing date.
Before You Apply

What You'll Need

  • Brand name and/or logo file (PNG/AI/PDF)
  • CNIC copy of the applicant / owner
  • Business registration proof, if applying as a company
  • List of goods/services the brand covers
  • Proof of prior use, if already trading (optional)
