Overview

Serve With Confidence, Operate Legally

Food businesses — from manufacturers to restaurants and packaged goods sellers — need proper licensing to demonstrate compliance with Pakistan's food safety regulations. It protects your customers, and it protects your business from closure risk.

Orakser handles the licensing application, coordinating facility, hygiene and labeling documentation so your food business is fully compliant.

Key Benefits

  • Legal authorization to manufacture or sell food
  • Reduces risk of closure or penalties
  • Builds consumer trust in your product safety
  • Often required for retail and distributor listings
  • Supports export readiness for food products
