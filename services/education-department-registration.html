Overview

Legal Standing for Your Institution

Schools, colleges, academies and training institutes need formal registration with the provincial Education Department to legally operate, issue recognized certificates, and enroll students with confidence.

Orakser guides founders and administrators through the registration requirements — facility standards, curriculum documentation, and staffing records — so your institution is fully compliant from day one.

Key Benefits

  • Legal authorization to operate as an institution
  • Enables recognized certification for students
  • Required for affiliation with boards/universities
  • Builds parent and student trust
  • Foundation for future expansion or franchising
