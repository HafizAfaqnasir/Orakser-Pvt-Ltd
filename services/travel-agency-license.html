Overview

Operate Legally, Book With Confidence

A licensed travel agency can legally issue tickets, packages and tour bookings, and is far more likely to be trusted by clients and partner airlines or hotels. Operating without proper licensing risks fines and blocked partnerships.

Orakser handles the licensing application with the relevant tourism authority, coordinating your office, staffing and financial documentation requirements.

Key Benefits

  • Legal authorization to operate as a travel agency
  • Required for IATA and airline partnership applications
  • Builds client and partner trust
  • Enables formal contracts with hotels and tour operators
  • Foundation for business bank facilities and credit lines
