Overview

Protect the Look That Sells Your Product

Design is one of the most valuable intellectual property assets a business can own — the visual form of a product can be just as crucial to its appeal as its function. Design registration protects the shape, configuration, pattern or ornamentation applied to an article.

Orakser assists manufacturers, packaging designers and product businesses in registering designs with IPO Pakistan, giving you exclusive rights to that visual identity.

Key Benefits

  • Exclusive rights to your product's visual design
  • Legal grounds against copycat products/packaging
  • Adds resale/licensing value to your product line
  • Complements trademark and patent protection
  • Faster and more affordable than patent protection

Before You Apply

What You'll Need

  • Drawings or photographs of the design (multiple views)
  • Description of the article the design applies to
  • CNIC copy of the applicant/owner
  • Business registration proof, if company-owned
  • Date of first use or disclosure, if any
Typically 6–12 months to registration
