Overview

Turn Your Invention Into a Protected Asset

A patent gives you the exclusive right to make, use and sell your invention for a fixed period — stopping others from commercially exploiting your idea without permission.

Orakser supports inventors, manufacturers and R&D teams through patentability evaluation, prior-art search, technical specification drafting, and full prosecution before the Intellectual Property Organization of Pakistan.

Common Questions

Patent FAQs

New, non-obvious and industrially applicable inventions — including processes, machines, and manufactured products — can generally be patented.

Abstract ideas, discoveries of natural phenomena, and purely aesthetic designs (which fall under design registration) typically cannot be patented.

Patent protection in Pakistan generally lasts 20 years from the filing date, subject to renewal fees.

Patent examination is detailed and can take several years depending on the technical field and any objections raised.
