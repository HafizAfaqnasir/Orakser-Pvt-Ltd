Overview

Unlock IT Export Benefits

PSEB registration is designed for IT and IT-enabled services companies exporting software or services — opening access to tax incentives, government support programs, and easier remittance handling for export earnings.

Orakser handles the registration process for software houses, freelancers and IT-enabled service providers, aligning your registration with your actual export activity.

Key Benefits

  • Access to IT export tax incentives
  • Easier foreign remittance handling for export income
  • Eligibility for government IT industry programs
  • Recognized status for international clients
  • Supports company credibility in bids and partnerships
