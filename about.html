Our Journey

From One Office To Nine

Fifteen years of steady, deliberate growth — always in step with the clients who trusted us first.

2010

Foundation in Karachi

Orakser (Orakzai Services) opens in Karachi to help Pakistani businesses register and protect trademarks, copyrights and patents.

2013

Registered IPO Pakistan Agents

We formalize our standing as registered agents with the Intellectual Property Organization of Pakistan and expand into Company & NTN registration.

2016

Expansion to Lahore & Islamabad

Growing demand from Punjab and the capital leads us to open dedicated offices in Lahore and Islamabad.

2019

5,000+ Clients, 3 New Cities

We cross 5,000 clients served and open offices in Faisalabad, Multan and Peshawar to stay close to our growing client base.

2022

Full-Service Business Registration

We broaden our offering to include licensing, franchising, domain registration and dedicated brand protection services.

Today

9 Offices, 10,000+ Clients

Orakser now serves clients from 9 offices across Pakistan and internationally — with the same standard of honest, prompt service we started with in 2010.
