Trademark, Company & Copyright Registration in Pakistan

Orakser (Orakzai Services) was founded in 2010 to make intellectual property and business registration simple for Pakistani businesses. We handle patent, design and trademark prosecution, licensing, clearance and validity searches, and litigation support.

Our team of lawyers and consultants works directly with IPO Pakistan, SECP and FBR, so you get honest advice, clear fees and one person who owns your case from first call to final certificate.

How It Works

From Idea to Certificate

A clear, transparent process — you always know exactly where your case stands.

  1. 1

    Searching

    We run a clearance search to check whether anyone already holds rights that could block your registration.

  2. 2

    Filing the Application

    We prepare and file your application with the right authority, with your details and full listing of goods or services.

  3. 3

    Examination

    The registry checks whether your mark or work is distinctive and compliant. We answer any objections for you.

  4. 4

    Publication for Opposition

    Accepted marks are published in the journal so others can object. We handle the opposition period.

  5. 5

    Certificate Issuance

    Once fees are paid the registration certificate is issued and you can use the ® symbol with your brand.
