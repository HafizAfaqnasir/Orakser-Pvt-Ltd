Trademark, Copyright & Company Registration Under One Roof

Orakser (Orakzai Services) handles intellectual property and business registration in Pakistan: trademark, patent and design prosecution, copyright, clearance searches, licensing and litigation support.

How It Works

From Idea To Certificate

A clear process. You always know which stage your application is at.

Search & Advice

We check your name against existing marks and tell you honestly if it can be registered.

Filing the Application

We prepare the documents and file with IPO Pakistan, SECP or the relevant authority.

Examination

The authority examines the application. We answer any objections on your behalf.

Publication

Your mark is published in the Trade Marks Journal so others can oppose. We monitor it.

Certificate Issued

You receive your registration certificate and your brand is legally protected.
