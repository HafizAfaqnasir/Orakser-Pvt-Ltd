Overview

Compliance That Keeps You on Shelves

PSQCA certification confirms that your product meets Pakistan Standards for quality and safety — mandatory for many regulated product categories before they can be legally sold or imported.

Orakser manages the testing coordination and documentation process with PSQCA, helping you avoid the delays that come from incomplete or misdirected applications.

Key Benefits

  • Required for many regulated product categories
  • Avoids customs and market seizure risk
  • Builds consumer trust in product safety
  • Smooths import clearance for regulated goods
  • Supports retail and distributor onboarding
